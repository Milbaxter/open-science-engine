Open Science Engine — backtest leaderboard

Ranking benchmark: every ranker scores the same pool of entity pairs never mentioned together with the disease before the cutoff. AUC = chance a pair that later got co-mentioned is ranked above one that didn't (0.5 = guessing).

Proposal benchmark: agents see only papers published before the cutoff and propose 20 hypotheses (A — relation — B). Novel: no earlier paper mentions the disease with both A and B. Emerged: a later paper does. Mech score = mean of novel × emerged × surprise (−log₁₀ chance co-occurrence). Judged replaces “emerged” with two LLM judges reading the later papers.
